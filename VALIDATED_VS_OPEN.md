## Status Definitions

- **Validated**: Prediction tested and confirmed with appropriate statistical rigor.
- **Validated-simulation**: Confirmed in simulation; awaiting real-data replication.
- **Validated-empirical**: Confirmed in real data but limited to one domain.
- **Validated-scoped**: Confirmed but only within a narrow scope.
- **Partial**: Some evidence exists but the full claim is not yet testable.
- **Not yet validated**: No empirical test has been conducted.
- **Open**: Actively being investigated; current evidence is mixed or absent.
